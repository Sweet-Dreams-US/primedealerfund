# Prime Dealer University — 2026 Blog Curriculum (May → Dec)

## Premise

The Prime Dealer Equity Fund insights library is structured as if Prime Dealer
University were teaching a master's program across four degrees:

1. **Automotive Dealership Operations** (18 disciplines)
2. **Private Equity & Fund Management** (14 disciplines)
3. **Sales & Marketing** (2 tracks, 25 disciplines)
4. **Business Leadership & Operations Management** (22 disciplines)

Each blog post is a **chapter**. The 43 posts published Jan 5 → May 1 covered
the foundational thesis chapters — why dealerships, recession resistance,
hard assets, generational transfer, co-investment basics, fund governance
overviews. The next 35 chapters move past introductions into the operational,
financial, and regulatory mechanics that institutional investors actually
underwrite.

## Publishing cadence

- **One post per week, every Wednesday**
- **35 weeks** (May 6, 2026 → December 30, 2026)
- All posts written in the same investor-grade tone as existing curriculum
- Each maps to one specific curriculum discipline, cited at the top of the
  draft
- **Scheduling:** Posts are written ahead of time and gated by their `date`
  field. The blog list and detail pages filter out any post whose date is
  in the future, so chapters auto-publish on their scheduled Wednesday
  without requiring a manual deploy each week.

## Production notes

- **File organization**: continue the monthly-split pattern. Create
  `blog-may-2026.ts`, `blog-june-2026.ts`, etc. (or quarterly: `blog-q3-2026.ts`).
- **Author rotation**: Kyle for operations chapters, Ralph for fund/legal
  chapters, joint by-line for strategy chapters.
- **Hero images**: rotate through the existing dealership photo library
  (no people photos per established rule).
- **Length**: target 8-12 content blocks per chapter, matching existing
  cadence. Heavier technical chapters (PPM, QoE, reinsurance) can run 14-16
  blocks with callouts.
- **Required callout types per chapter**:
  - At least one `callout-numbers` (the "by the numbers" stat block)
  - At least one `subheading` every ~3 paragraphs (drives the table-of-contents
    sidebar that we built)
- **Compliance disclaimer**: every post should retain the standard
  "informational only / not an offer / accredited investors only" footer
  language we drafted in the disclaimer review.

## What this does NOT include

- Press releases for new acquisitions (those are timed to actual closings,
  not the curriculum schedule).
- Earnings/distribution updates (those are quarterly investor letters,
  separate from the public blog).
- Reactive content (industry news, market commentary, OEM announcements) —
  those would slot in as bonus posts on Mondays or Fridays without
  disrupting the Wednesday curriculum cadence.
